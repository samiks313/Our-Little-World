A Letter to My Love

Things I may not say perfectly every day, but will always feel.

To my dearest love, ❤️

Sometimes I look at you and wonder how I got so lucky. Out of all the people in this world, life gave me you — the person I get to love, annoy, laugh with, and grow old with.

You may not even realise how much the little things mean to me. The way you care, the way you notice things, and the way you make me feel loved without needing a special occasion. Those are the moments my heart remembers the most.

I know we won't have perfect days every day. We may disagree, get tired, or have moments when life feels difficult. But I don't just love you for the beautiful days. I want to stand beside you on the difficult ones too.

I want to watch us grow — celebrate our little wins, build our dreams, make a home full of laughter, collect memories in every place we go, and one day look back at our life and smile at how far we've come together.

I want more morning smiles, more silly conversations, more hugs after a long day, more adventures, and more ordinary days that become extraordinary just because we're together.

You are my safe place, my favourite part of every ordinary day, and the person I want beside me in every version of my future.

If I had to live this life a thousand times, I would still hope to find you in every single one.

When we're older, with a thousand memories behind us, I hope I'll still hold your hand, look at you the same way, and feel that same little happiness in my heart: “It's still you. It has always been you.”

I love you more than this little letter could ever explain.

Always and endlessly yours,
Your Samiksha ❤️
